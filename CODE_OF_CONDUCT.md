# Code of Conduct

StructureCraft welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone participating in this repository is expected to help keep discussion respectful and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and assume good intent while taking concerns seriously.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give specific, useful feedback and accept corrections respectfully.
- Respect other people's privacy, time, and boundaries.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption, and publishing someone else's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the repository maintainer through an available private contact method. If none is listed, open an issue asking for a private contact channel without naming affected people or including sensitive details. Security vulnerabilities follow the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. Decisions should consider the context and severity of the behavior. Do not retaliate against someone for raising a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces maintained for StructureCraft. It does not imply that maintainers can moderate services or spaces outside their control.
